What's in it

  • Every offense in the Penal, Vehicle, and Health & Safety Codes, and the Business & Professions, Government, and Welfare & Institutions offenses in the DOJ offense table.
  • Felony triads and life terms, prison or county jail under 1170(h), misdemeanor maximums, and base fines.
  • Serious, violent, and strike status, conduct credits, probation bars, PC 290 tier, and firearm prohibition.
  • Enhancements, including 12022.53, 12022.7, 667(a), and 186.22.
  • Degree, value, and finding-dependent offenses show each alternative with its own cite. Examples: 459, 487, 594, 187, 209.
  • Full statute text, CALCRIM cross-references, and recent amendments.
  • Custody credit, court day, and release date calculators.

How the data is kept current

Statute text comes from the Legislature's official data (leginfo). Each rebuild is checked against a hand-verified set of offenses before it ships, and the app downloads new law in the background. When the data is older than the next January 1 effective date, the app says so.

Not legal advice

Chargebook is a reference tool for legal professionals. Values are derived from statute text and can be wrong for a particular case. Offense date, priors, enhancements, and case law all matter. Read the cited section before relying on any value.
