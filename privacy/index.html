Privacy

Effective November 9, 2026

Chargebook collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

On your device

Searches, saved offenses, recent items, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Law updates

The app downloads updated law data from our update server. That request carries no information about you or your searches. As with any web server, the server's access log records the requesting IP address and time. We use these logs only to run and protect the server, and they are deleted within 14 days.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Email

If you email us, including through Report a Problem, we use your message and address only to answer you.
