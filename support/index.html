To report a wrong value, long-press the row on the offense card and choose Report a Problem. The report names the offense, row, and data version. Include the section that says otherwise.

Common questions

A value is marked "Unreviewed"

It was derived automatically from statute text and hasn't been checked by an attorney yet. Read the cited section before relying on it.

"Punishment not determined"

The statute doesn't state a punishment that could be derived reliably. Tap the row to read the section.

How current is the law?

Settings and the bottom of every offense card show "Law current through" and the data version. The app checks for new data in the background. When a January 1 effective date passes without an update, a banner says the data may be out of date.

Restore a subscription on a new phone

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Chargebook. Access continues to the end of the period you paid for.
